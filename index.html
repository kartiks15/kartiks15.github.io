Lab notes

Can a smaller model cost more energy to run? For 4-bit Mistral-7B, my early numbers say yes.

B.Tech thesis · in progress

FP4 vs NF4: what 4-bit quantization costs in energy

With two teammates and advised by Prof. Rajesh Verma, I'm comparing the two 4-bit post-training quantization formats on energy use as well as accuracy.

Model
Mistral-7B in FP16, FP4 and NF4
Hardware
NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000
Quality
MMLU, WikiText-2 perplexity, hidden-state similarity to FP16
Cost
Throughput, and GPU power from NVML (idle-subtracted, PUE-scaled)
Protocol
Five trials in randomized order

Findings so far

  1. 4-bit cuts peak GPU memory from 14.5 GB to about 4.6 GB and raises throughput from 71.1 to about 90 tokens/s.
  2. It doesn't save energy. A full MMLU run uses about 5% more than FP16: 0.104 kWh (FP4) and 0.105 kWh (NF4) against 0.0994.
  3. NF4 beats FP4 on accuracy (0.491 vs 0.487), perplexity and hidden-state similarity, and uses slightly more energy.

Capstone · in progress

A matmul kernel that draws less power

Matrix multiplication dominates LLM inference cost, so any saving there carries into every model in the thesis. I'm writing my own matmul kernel in Triton aimed at lower GPU power draw, and I'll measure it with the same NVML harness so its numbers compare directly with the thesis results.
